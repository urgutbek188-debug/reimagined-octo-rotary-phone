The Setup · 5 Minutes

Turn Your Phone Into a Focus Machine

On iPhone

1

Create a "Work" Focus

Settings → Focus → + → Custom. Name it "Work."

2

Allow almost no one

Under People, allow only 1–3 emergency contacts. Silence every other app — especially social, news & games.

3

Put it on a schedule

Set a Schedule → your work hours, Mon–Fri. Now it arms itself every day automatically.

On Android

1

Open Digital Wellbeing

Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Focus mode. (Tip: search Settings for "Digital Wellbeing.")

2

Pause your distracting apps

Check every app that pulls you off task. Focus mode greys them out during work hours.

3

Set a schedule

Tap Set a schedule → your work hours. Done — it runs on its own.

✓Do This Now (2 minutes)
  • Build the "Work" Focus / Focus mode on your phone using the steps above.
  • Whitelist 3 people max. Silence everything else.
  • Set the schedule so it turns on by itself tomorrow morning.

Bonus · 60 Seconds

The 60-Second Desk Reset

Before each focus session, run this once. It removes the triggers your shielded phone can't:

  • Phone in another room. Not a drawer — a different room. Distance is friction, and friction protects you.
  • Close every non-essential browser tab. Each open tab is an open loop pulling at your attention.
  • Write your ONE task on paper. One clear target kills the urge to wander.

That's it. Phone shielded, desk reset — you've just removed 90% of what was stealing your focus.
